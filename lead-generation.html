Where the work leaks out

The gap is almost never at the top of the funnel.

Before we talk about spending more on advertising, we follow the enquiries you already get. In most reviews the loss is in the first hour and the third follow-up — not in the number of people who got in touch.

Capture and reply

Nothing arrives without an owner.

Enquiries turn up as form fills, phone calls, referrals, DMs and ad clicks, and each one usually lands somewhere different. We bring them into one place, acknowledge them straight away and put a name against each.

  • Every channel into one enquiry list
  • Automatic acknowledgement within minutes
  • Routed to the right person, with context
  • Missed calls captured and returned to the queue
  • No enquiry sitting unowned overnight
Speed of first reply is the single change that moves conversion most in the businesses we review. It is also the cheapest thing to fix, which is why we start there rather than with ad spend.
Qualify and follow up

The third follow-up is where the work is.

Most quotes are lost to silence, not to a competitor. We build the follow-up so it keeps going politely and predictably until you get an answer either way — and so you can see exactly where each job sits.

  • Qualification questions asked up front
  • Follow-up sequences until you get a yes or a no
  • Quotes chased on a schedule, not on memory
  • Pipeline visible in one view
  • Conversion reported by source
We do not buy, scrape or rent lead lists, and we will not quote you a number of leads per month. What we commit to is that every enquiry you generate is captured, answered and followed up.
