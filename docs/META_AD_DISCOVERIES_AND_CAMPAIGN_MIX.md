# Run Lighter Meta advertising discoveries and campaign mix

Updated: 27 July 2026

## 1. Purpose

This document consolidates the practical advertising lessons gathered from:

- The Run Lighter brand and business doctrine
- Existing Run Lighter organic and paid creative
- The current lead campaign
- Australian competitor advertising reviewed in Meta Ad Library
- The owner's direct reactions to the ads and creative
- Three independently developed campaign concepts

It is the working source of truth for the next Meta lead-generation test.

## 2. Fixed Run Lighter doctrine

Run Lighter helps growing, owner-led Sydney businesses remove repeated digital work by designing and building practical automation around the tools they already use.

The advertising must preserve these principles:

- Lead with a recognisable business problem, not AI.
- Sell an operational improvement, not a technology package.
- Human judgement, relationships and accountability stay human.
- Improve and connect existing software before replacing it.
- Start with one painful process and prove it.
- Do not position staff removal as the benefit.
- Do not use profit claims, invented savings, case studies or statistics.
- Keep text on static artwork to seven words or fewer.
- Use grounded Australian settings, tactile metaphors and useful diagrams.
- Avoid robots, holograms, glowing brains, futuristic dashboards and generic AI imagery.
- Use the earthy Run Lighter palette: moss, eucalyptus, sage, oat, warm paper and stone.
- Use no em dashes in public-facing copy.

Primary offer:

**Free On-Site Automation Review for suitable Sydney businesses**

Primary business outcome:

**Your business should not need you everywhere.**

## 3. What the current campaign has taught us

The live campaign has used the direct proposition:

**We destroy data entry.**

This is stronger than broad automation language because it:

- Names a familiar enemy
- Can be understood without knowing anything about AI
- Creates a clear visual idea
- Applies across several service sectors
- Gives the caption room to explain CRM, email, spreadsheets and finance-system handoffs

The campaign has already produced leads. That establishes message relevance, but it does not yet establish a scalable winner. The next test must judge lead quality, not just form volume.

Current operational constraints:

- Initial budget is approximately AUD $10 per day.
- Meta instant forms are intentionally short.
- The form collects contact details first.
- Qualification happens in a fast personal follow-up.
- The preferred sales step is a brief call followed by an on-site review for suitable Sydney businesses.

Implication:

At AUD $10 per day, do not fragment delivery across many ad sets. Use one Sydney ad set with a controlled set of meaningfully different ads. Let each creative call out a specific buyer or problem. Move a concept into its own ad set only after it proves it can attract suitable leads.

## 4. Owner feedback that must shape the next test

The strongest competitor ads made the intended buyer immediately think, “this is for me”.

Specific observations:

- Trade ads work when they name the trade owner and describe the work they did not enter the trade to perform.
- Paradino stands out because it is calm, specific and purpose-built for financial advisers rather than presenting generic AI services.
- Broad AI imagery and broad automation copy feel interchangeable.
- Dense black-and-green posters are direct but visually weak at mobile size.
- One recognisable pain is stronger than a long list of automation capabilities.
- The visual must stop the scroll without becoming bright, stressful or off-brand.
- Strong creative can be strange, tactile or metaphorical, as long as the proposition remains immediately clear.
- Sector-specific variants are worth testing for trades, accountants, advisers, legal practices and booking-heavy hospitality businesses.

## 5. Australian competitor review

Public Meta Ad Library data does not reveal conversion rate, lead quality, cost per acquisition or return on ad spend. Ad count, duplication and time active are useful testing signals only.

### Closest strategic benchmarks

| Advertiser | What it does well | What Run Lighter should avoid |
|---|---|---|
| BizFlow Automation | Specific operational pains, founder-led delivery, long-running test system | Unsupported savings claims and generic consulting language |
| Pipereply | Self-demonstrating 60-second call offer, restrained green-and-cream creative, one clear failure point | Becoming too narrowly associated with missed-call software |
| Paradino | Calm, premium, sector-specific positioning for Australian financial advisers | Copying its category or corporate visual identity |
| Tradies AI | Names the buyer, qualifies capacity and uses native founder video | Overheated growth claims and long direct-response videos without proof |
| Tech Automation | Speaks directly to tradies and names the admin they dislike | Dense poster graphics, cluttered offer and profit-first language |
| Supercharged AI | Frames the problem as business design rather than another tool | Abstract reinvention language and synthetic office imagery |
| Hunt St | Plain-spoken owner scenarios and a clear human alternative | Confusing outsourcing with Run Lighter's automation offer |
| Campaign Brain | One enemy, one productised alternative and a simple price comparison | Aggressive “fire your agency” language that does not fit Run Lighter |
| LeftclickTech | Direct administrative pain and clear free-audit offer | Text-heavy graphics and unverified numerical promises |
| Lilley Media | Tight audience specificity and an easy-to-understand commercial mechanism | Results cards and claims Run Lighter cannot yet substantiate |

### Durable category lessons

1. **Specific beats broad.** “Automation for business” is weak. “Your best accountants are copying client data” is recognisable.
2. **The mechanism matters.** Show what changes between enquiry, handover, follow-up, reporting or invoicing.
3. **A demonstration is stronger than a promise.** The first step should let the prospect see their own workflow clearly.
4. **Founder visibility builds trust.** Use it selectively, without turning every ad into a talking-head video.
5. **One offer should anchor the test.** Change audience, hook and visual before changing the offer.
6. **Lead quality outranks cheap leads.** The cheapest instant form is not useful if the business is too small, outside Sydney or not ready to implement.
7. **Calm can still stop the scroll.** Surprise can come from the object, framing or proposition rather than neon colour.

## 6. Recommended low-budget Meta structure

### Campaign

**Objective:** Leads
**Conversion location:** Meta instant form
**Budget:** AUD $10 per day
**Geography:** Sydney and practical on-site travel radius
**Age:** Broad working-age owner and decision-maker range
**Placements:** Advantage+ placements, with every asset checked in Feed, Stories and Reels

### Ad set

Use one broad Sydney ad set during the first test. Do not try to place different targeting rules on individual ads inside it. The creative performs the first layer of audience selection.

### Ads

Run no more than three active concepts at once:

1. One broad operational problem
2. One sector callout
3. One challenger or curiosity concept

Keep the offer, form and follow-up process consistent so the creative is the main variable.

### Form

Keep the Meta form short:

- Name
- Phone
- Email

Use the greeting and ad copy to state the fit:

- Sydney business
- Established team
- Interested in removing repeated work

Qualify personally during the first short call.

### Measurement order

1. Qualified conversations
2. Suitable on-site reviews
3. Completed lead forms
4. Cost per qualified conversation
5. Cost per lead
6. Click-through rate

### Decision rules

- Do not declare a winner from one lead.
- Do not kill a concept because of weak clicks before it has received meaningful delivery.
- Pause obvious low-quality lead sources quickly.
- Record the audience, hook, visual and form version against every lead.
- Promote a winning sector concept into its own ad set only after it repeatedly produces suitable conversations.

- The language is memorable.
- It works across multiple sectors.
- It can support unusual, tactile creative.

### Owner dependency

**Artwork:** Your business should not need you everywhere.

Why it stays:

Why it stays:

- It is easy to adapt for trades, sales-led services and booking-heavy businesses.
- The workflow can be explained concretely.

## 8. Three new independently developed campaigns

The three campaign sections below are commissioned as distinct strategic options. Each must use a different audience, emotional trigger and creative mechanism.

### Campaign A: Your Business Followed You Home

#### Strategic role

An emotional recognition campaign for owners of Sydney professional-service firms with approximately 10 to 50 staff.

Priority sectors:

- Accounting and financial services
- Mortgage and property advisory
- Recruitment
- Legal and consulting firms
- Marketing and creative agencies

#### Insight

The owner rarely says they need AI. They notice that customer follow-up, approvals, reporting and system updates still reach them after hours.

Competitors frequently sell saved hours, reduced cost or headcount leverage. This campaign owns the more personal moment when a supposedly established business follows the owner home.

#### Creative hook

**YOUR BUSINESS FOLLOWED YOU HOME.**

#### Primary text

You can build a good service business and still spend your evenings checking follow-ups, chasing approvals and moving information between systems.

That work usually points to a process problem, not a people problem.

Run Lighter visits Sydney businesses, maps the repeated work and identifies what could be automated without removing human judgement.

Leave your details for a short 10-minute call. If there is a useful fit, we will arrange a free on-site automation review.

#### Meta fields

- **Headline:** Book an On-Site Automation Review
- **Description:** Practical automation for Sydney service businesses.
- **CTA:** Learn More

#### Lead form

- **Type:** Higher Intent
- **Intro:** Find what keeps following you home
- **Fields:** Full name, work email and mobile number
- **Completion:** Adrian will call for a short conversation. Suitable businesses can then arrange a free on-site review.

#### Creative system

The control image is a dining table set for the evening, but one place is occupied by an office chair, laptop, customer forms and an overflowing follow-up folder. No person appears. The absent owner is implied.

Use warm evening light, bone cream, deep forest green, charcoal and natural timber.

Variants:

1. **The Empty Place:** Office work has physically occupied one place at the dinner table.
2. **The Paper Trail:** Business paperwork crosses the front threshold and continues into the home.
3. **Clock Off:** A quiet office closes while a clean physical workflow continues moving.

#### Why it is distinct

It sells relief from owner intrusion rather than AI, cost reduction or abstract efficiency. The domestic boundary makes the problem emotionally legible without distressed stock photography.

### Campaign B: The Repeated Detail

#### Strategic role

A practical professional-services campaign that turns double handling into a visible, tactile process defect.

Primary audiences:

- Accountants and advisers
- Recruitment and property services
- Agencies and consultancies
- Specialist B2B service firms

#### Insight

Business owners rarely ask for automation. They notice that the same enquiry, customer detail or job information keeps being typed, copied and checked by different people.

#### Creative hook

**HOW MANY TIMES DID YOU TYPE THAT?**

#### Primary text

One client detail. Five systems. Three people typing it again.

If that sounds familiar, there is probably a cleaner way to move work through your business.

Run Lighter visits Sydney service businesses, maps one repetitive workflow and shows where automation could remove double handling while keeping decisions with your team.

We work around the software you already use. No generic AI pitch and no obligation.

Leave your details and Adrian will call to arrange a short conversation.

#### Meta fields

- **Headline:** Find the repeated work in your business
- **Description:** Free on-site automation review for Sydney businesses.
- **CTA:** Learn More

#### Lead form

- **Type:** Higher Intent
- **Intro:** Find the work worth removing
- **Fields:** Full name, work email and mobile number
- **Completion:** Adrian will call within one business day for a short conversation about how work currently moves through the business.

#### Creative system

Use an overhead premium editorial still life on a warm cream surface. One piece of customer information appears repeatedly across a chain of physical office objects. The repeated detail is understood before any interface is shown.

Variants:

1. **Carbon Copies:** Identical customer cards move through a green channel and accumulate unnecessarily.
2. **The Rubber Stamp:** One customer card is surrounded by repeated stamp impressions. A cropped hand is about to stamp it again.
3. **The Loop:** A physical folder travels along a green track that returns to its starting point while duplicate pages spill out.

#### Why it is distinct

It stays practical and industry-flexible while avoiding generic software screenshots. It can become the next challenger to “We destroy data entry” without repeating the same destructive visual language.

### Campaign C: The Owner Bottleneck

#### Strategic role

A trade and field-service campaign for owners of Sydney businesses with an established team.

Priority sectors:

- Electrical
- Plumbing
- HVAC
- Maintenance
- Building services
- Specialist contractors

#### Insight

The owner does not usually describe the problem as an inefficient workflow. They say:

- Everything still comes back to me.
- The team cannot move until I answer.
- I am always the one who remembers.
- I hired people, but I am still in every job.

The deeper problem is that the owner remains the operating system.

#### Creative hook

**EVERYTHING WAITS FOR THE OWNER**

#### Primary text

Everything still comes back to you.

The quote. The schedule change. The customer follow-up. The approval someone is waiting on.

That does not always mean you need more staff. It may mean routine work has nowhere else to go.

Run Lighter visits Sydney service businesses, maps the repeated work that keeps returning to the owner, and identifies where practical automation can keep it moving without removing human judgement.

Book a free On-Site Automation Review. You will leave with an Owner Bottleneck Map and one clear place to start.

#### Meta fields

- **Headline:** Find Your Owner Bottleneck
- **Description:** Free on-site review for Sydney service businesses.
- **CTA:** Book Now

#### Lead form

- **Type:** Higher Intent
- **Intro:** Find the work waiting on you
- **Fields:** Full name, work email and mobile number
- **Completion:** Adrian will contact the lead for a 10-minute fit call. Suitable businesses can then book the free on-site review.

#### Creative system

Use a premium, slightly surreal photographic metaphor. Ordinary business objects are physically unable to move because they all depend on one central owner object.

Variants:

1. **The Tied Chair:** An empty owner chair is pinned by cords connected to folders, a desk phone, job sheets and a calendar.
2. **The Master Key:** One worn brass key carries an absurdly large ring of keys, access tags and approval stamps.
3. **The Waiting Line:** Job folders, a ringing phone, keys and an unsigned quote form a literal queue toward an absent owner desk.

#### Why it is distinct

It avoids the crowded “AI receptionist for tradies” category and owns a more strategic trade-owner problem. The business has grown, but routine work still cannot move without the owner.

## 9. Recommended first test mix

All three new campaigns belong in the campaign library, but AUD $10 per day is not enough to test them all simultaneously with useful delivery.

Use a sequential challenger system:

### Days 1 to 7

- **Control:** We destroy data entry.
- **Challenger:** Everything waits for the owner.

This compares a broad operational enemy with a trade-owner dependency problem.

### Days 8 to 14

- Keep the better qualified-lead producer.
- Introduce **Your business followed you home.**

This tests a professional-services emotional angle against the first-stage winner.

### Days 15 to 21

- Keep the current leader.
- Introduce **How many times did you type that?**

This tests a calmer, practical double-handling concept against the surviving control.

Use one campaign, one Sydney ad set, one form and no more than two active ads at any time. Each ad can call out a different buyer in its copy. Individual ads cannot have separate audiences inside the same ad set.

Compare campaigns against:

- Audience fit
- Immediate clarity
- Creative stopping power
- Distinctiveness from current ads
- Ability to work at AUD $10 per day
- Lead quality potential
- Fit with the on-site review
- Credibility without case studies

Initial decision thresholds:

- Give an individual creative at least AUD $20 or approximately 1,000 impressions before judging it, unless it is clearly broken or attracting irrelevant enquiries.
- Pause a creative after approximately AUD $35 to A$50 without a lead when its response is also materially weaker than the competing creative.
- Review the full offer and form if approximately AUD $120 has been spent without a valid Sydney business lead.
- Do not increase the daily budget until at least two leads have been confirmed as genuine owner or decision-maker conversations.

These are operating thresholds for a new account, not permanent performance benchmarks.

## 11. Email marketing decision

Brevo is the preferred MVP platform.

Reasons:

- A usable free tier for the launch stage
- Marketing campaigns, contact management and automation in one account
- API and SMTP options for future website and workflow integration
- Domain authentication support
- Suitable for a small consultancy without committing to a monthly platform fee

MVP configuration:

- Sender: Run Lighter
- Intended address: `adrian@runlighter.com`
- Incoming mail forwarded to the owner's existing Gmail
- Brevo used for subscriber lists, campaign sends and simple automations
- SPF, DKIM and DMARC configured after the domain purchase
- No paid Google Workspace subscription during the MVP stage

The physical business address required in commercial email footers must be supplied accurately by the owner. It must not be invented.
