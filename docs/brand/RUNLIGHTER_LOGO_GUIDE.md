# Run Lighter logo system V2

## Primary lockup

The approved construction is a compact, left-aligned two-line wordmark:

```text
Run/
Lighter
```

The first line is intentionally dominant. The second line is calmer, not fragile. Both lines have been optically sized to form one balanced block rather than two unrelated words.

## Typography

The artwork is based on outlined **Avenir Next** letterforms:

- `Run`: Avenir Next Heavy
- `Lighter`: Avenir Next Medium
- `/`: custom-drawn forward-motion slash

Avenir Next is geometric enough to feel systematic and capable, but its humanist proportions stop the consultancy from looking like a software product or an AI startup. Its open counters remain clear at small sizes. The single-storey `g` gives the lower line a cleaner rhythm and removes the visual knot created by the previous double-storey form.

The weight contrast expresses the name without sacrificing reproduction. `Run` carries action and confidence. `Lighter` carries ease and clarity, but stays substantial enough for mobile headers, social profiles and proposal covers.

## Slash

The slash is a bespoke parallelogram rather than a font character. It is sized against the stems in `Run` and aligned to the cap height and baseline. It functions as both punctuation and a visual handoff: work moves forward, then becomes lighter.

Do not rotate, shorten, separate or replace the slash with a typed character.

## Colour

Primary palette:

- Ink: `#29312C`
- Eucalyptus accent: `#587664`
- Oat field: `#F3EFE5`
- Paper: `#FBF8F1`
- Moss reverse field: `#344137`

The primary transparent artwork uses ink lettering and the eucalyptus slash. The one-colour version is provided for stamps, embroidery, laser cutting and restricted production. The reverse version uses paper lettering with a pale eucalyptus slash on moss.

## Spacing and scale

- Preserve the supplied line spacing and optical alignment.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least half the height of the lowercase `u` in `Run`.
- Recommended minimum digital width: 120 px.
- Use the supplied 64 px and 256 px files for small-size checks, not as master artwork.
- Use SVG whenever possible. Use the 2400 px PNGs where raster artwork is required.

## Do not

- Do not retype the logo.
- Do not stretch, compress or alter the line widths.
- Do not add a badge, monogram, shadow, gradient, outline or slogan.
- Do not recolour individual letters.
- Do not place the primary dark version on a busy photograph.
- Do not use the discarded development directions as live logos.
