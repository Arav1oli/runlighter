# Run Lighter daily commercial content brief

Updated: 15 August 2026

This is the controlling editorial brief for the single daily Run Lighter content scheduler. Read it before selecting a topic or generating an image. The live website, recent delivery evidence, Adrian's latest feedback and the publication history are inputs to the brief. An existing content plan is not authority to repeat a weak direction.

## The commercial job

Create one piece of marketing that makes a qualified Sydney owner recognise a valuable problem Run Lighter can help solve. The package must increase the perceived range and usefulness of Run Lighter, not make the business look like a one-problem workflow consultancy.

The public website is the minimum service map, not the limit of Run Lighter's real capability. Every run must inspect the current site and recent verified delivery evidence before ideation. Additional capability may be marketed only when it is supported by real Run Lighter work, repository evidence or explicit owner direction. Do not invent services, proof or outcomes.

## Minimum service map from the live website

### Business automation and workflow systems

1. Lead capture and follow-up
2. Client onboarding and handovers
3. Administration and data movement
4. Reporting and visibility

### Managed marketing and lead systems

5. Paid acquisition and lead handling
6. Website and local search priorities
7. Articles, social and email publishing
8. Campaign reporting and improvement

### Cross-service capability

The site also establishes that Run Lighter can:

- trace real work on site before recommending software
- connect existing software rather than defaulting to replacement
- build one controlled improvement, prove it and expand carefully
- join campaigns to lead follow-up, administration and reporting
- preserve human judgement, access control, relationships and accountability

These are mechanisms and trust signals, not a ninth topic to repeat every day.

## Coverage and rotation rules

Before generating ideas, audit the previous 30 days by service family, buyer problem, service mechanism, visual archetype and buyer sector.

- Prefer a commercially valuable service family that has been absent or poorly represented.
- A rolling seven successful posts must cover at least four distinct service families, including at least two from managed marketing and at least two from business automation.
- A rolling fourteen successful posts should cover all eight website service lines unless current evidence or explicit owner direction justifies a recorded exception.
- Do not publish more than two consecutive packages about owner bottlenecks, stuck work, conflicting rules, approvals or handoffs.
- Do not treat a new prop, industry or article question as a new idea when the underlying proposition remains "the process is messy, so automate the routine steps".
- The rolling-four professional-services audience rule still applies, but audience rotation does not substitute for service rotation.
- Record the chosen service family, coverage gap and rejected repetitive themes in the daily audit.

The site taxonomy is a floor. When verified recent work proves another genuine capability, add it to the candidate pool and explain the evidence. Never imply that the examples already marketed are everything Run Lighter does.

## Idea generation

Generate at least eight materially different commercial propositions before choosing one. They must differ in the problem, buyer consequence, service mechanism and visual event, not merely in wording.

Each candidate needs:

- a named service family
- a specific qualified buyer
- an expensive or frustrating consequence
- the Run Lighter work that changes the situation
- the judgement or relationship that stays human
- a decisive visible event
- a hook of seven words or fewer

Reject any candidate whose service connection is vague or whose image could advertise an unrelated consultancy.

## Hard visual gate

The image must stop a thumb and communicate an idea. "Operationally specific" is not enough.

Run the mute test before building the article:

1. Reduce the creative to roughly 20 per cent size.
2. Look for one second without the caption.
3. Ask what a Sydney business owner would say is happening.
4. Ask what commercial consequence they would feel.
5. Ask whether the hook changes or sharpens the image rather than explaining a weak image.

Fail the concept if the answer is only a list of objects, a location or a process description.

Hard-reject:

- passive still lifes of documents, keys, folders, cabinets, clipboards, desks or equipment
- decorative Australian cues that do not carry the commercial idea
- generic offices, posed staff, stock-like business scenes or screenshots presented as creativity
- another abstract "work is stuck" metaphor
- a photograph that depends on the caption to become relevant
- technically polished imagery with no surprise, humour, jeopardy, contrast, motion or emotional consequence
- a safe composition chosen mainly because it is easy to generate and brand

Australian context is a supporting quality, not a pass mark. Use it when the place, behaviour, humour, light, object or setting makes the proposition more truthful and distinctive.

Strong mechanisms may include a decisive human action, a visible before-and-after contrast, an unexpected scale shift, controlled visual humour, a physical demonstration, a high-stakes moment, an editorial comparison or another device that makes the consequence instantly felt. Photography is not mandatory. The best format is the one that carries the idea most clearly on a phone.

## Selection score

Score each candidate out of 100:

- commercial consequence: 20
- buyer and service fit: 20
- one-second visual meaning: 20
- stopping power and emotional energy: 15
- novelty against the last 30 days: 15
- mobile legibility and brand fit: 10

Minimum selection score: 85. A candidate also fails if it scores below 14/20 for commercial consequence, buyer and service fit or one-second visual meaning. Do not average away a fatal weakness.

The audit must preserve all candidate scores and one plain-English reason each rejected concept failed.

## Package standard

The selected proposition controls the social creative, caption, article, hero, Open Graph image and CTA. Do not make the article and image about adjacent ideas.

- Main creative copy: seven words or fewer.
- Caption: concise, situation first, one service mechanism, one human boundary and one direct CTA.
- Article: 700 to 1,300 words, answer first and genuinely useful.
- Creative: 1080 by 1350 with mobile contrast and the exact readable disclosure.
- Hero and Open Graph assets: derived from the same proposition, not a generic crop.
- Alt text: describe the meaningful visual event, not the marketing claim.
- No invented results, statistics, testimonials, clients or proof.
- No em dashes. Use Australian English.

## Final pre-publication challenge

Immediately before staging, answer these questions in the audit:

1. Which live Run Lighter service does this sell?
2. What capability does it add to the recent public story?
3. Is it materially different from the last seven posts?
4. What does the image communicate in one second without the caption?
5. Why would a qualified owner care now?
6. Would Adrian describe the image as engaging rather than merely competent?

Any weak or generic answer is a hard stop. Return to ideation. Do not publish a complete but dull package to satisfy the schedule.
