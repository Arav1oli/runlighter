# Run Lighter creative rebuild

Created: 20 July 2026

## Why the first batch failed

The first batch repeated one layout ten times. The diagrams changed, but the viewer experienced the same headline tile on every post. It lacked visual tension, audience specificity, storytelling and native social energy.

## Creative rules for the replacement batch

- Build for a 4:5 mobile feed canvas, with a 9:16 adaptation path for Stories and Reels.
- Give every post one dominant visual idea and one focal point.
- Keep artwork copy at seven words or fewer.
- Use different creative mechanisms, not recoloured templates.
- Make the business problem understandable before the caption is read.
- Keep the Run Lighter palette consistent: moss, eucalyptus, sage, oat, paper and stone.
- Use human presence sparingly. Hands, traces of work and real environments are preferable to generic stock portraits.
- Keep the tone practical, premium, grounded and Australian.
- Avoid robots, holograms, glowing brains, sci-fi interfaces, neon colour, orange, invented logos, profit claims and distressed stock-photo poses.

## Caption principles

- Two short paragraphs plus one direct CTA or question.
- Start with the situation the owner recognises.
- Explain the operational change in plain English.
- Avoid generic AI hype, long hashtag blocks and invented outcomes.
- Use one to three relevant hashtags only.

## Meta implementation notes

- Feed master: 1080 by 1350 pixels, 4:5.
- Stories and Reels adaptation: 1080 by 1920 pixels, 9:16, key copy inside the safe zone.
- Keep primary text concise, normally two or three lines before expansion.
- Test substantially different concepts and audience problems.
- Use carousel when a post promises a numbered list or explanation.
- Preview every placement before publishing.

## Research basis

Meta's current guidance supports the changes made here:

- Photo ads should have one focal point, minimal text, visual consistency and high-resolution imagery: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/photo-ad-format
- Feed imagery should be framed for mobile at 1:1 or 4:5, and primary copy should stay concise: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/automation/tailored-campaigns
- Creative diversification should test meaningfully different messages and formats: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/performance-marketing
- Native Reels creative should use 9:16 video, audio and safe-zone messaging: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads

Comparable Australian automation businesses and public social content were also reviewed, including DBPA, Automata AI, Aivy and Sumit Brands. Their strongest material uses a sharp operational point of view and concrete workflow language. The wider category commonly repeats terminal graphics, generic AI networks, abstract dashboards and long technology lists. Run Lighter should distinguish itself with tangible business scenes, practical metaphors and selective use of AI language.
