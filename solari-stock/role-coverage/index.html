2026-2027 | PLAN IMPLEMENTATION MARKETING AND BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT STRATEGY

How the accepted plan will be implemented

Solari & Stock is seeking practical ownership of marketing, business development, referral relationships, publishing, campaigns and performance reporting. The proposed service supplies that operating function without requiring another full-time in-house appointment.

The accepted Marketing and Business Development Strategy 2026-2027 remains the strategic authority. Our role is to organise, prepare, edit, publish, manage and measure the work required to put its marketing and acquisition priorities into operation.

01

What the external service will manage

Each workstream supports the accepted plan while keeping legal and relationship ownership with the firm.

Business development and referralsMaintain the professional contact register, priority referrer groups and follow-up programme.Expand

Prepare outreach and meeting material, schedule follow-up, support agreed events and record referred enquiries and matters. Directors and lawyers continue to own the relationships. We make the programme organised, consistent and visible.

Marketing strategy and campaignsMaintain the master marketing calendar and turn agreed priorities into scheduled work.Expand

Build and manage Google Search, Facebook and Instagram campaigns, starting with the Separation Options Consult, and improve advertisements, audiences, landing pages, booking paths and follow-up using actual results.

Content, newsletters and social publishingPrepare solicitor briefs, edit approved legal content, create images and publish across channels.Expand

Publish website material and repurpose it across LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram and email. Produce the monthly client newsletter, quarterly professional and referrer newsletter, targeted professional outreach and agreed guides or brochures.

Reporting and client acquisition trackingConnect available activity and enquiry data in one live dashboard.Expand

Connect data from the website, local search, paid campaigns, email, social activity, enquiries and matter outcomes. Track the path from enquiry to consultation to opened matter and known revenue where the information is available.

Website and local searchOwn the website work list and coordinate hosting, development and technical suppliers.Expand

Improve Family Law and Wills and Estates service pages, local search coverage, the Google Business Profile, internal links, page information and enquiry paths. Preserve effective existing work while removing duplication and fixing proven gaps.

Coordination and delivery controlMaintain one live work list covering priorities, owners, drafts, approvals and next actions.Expand

Keep publication dates and campaign status visible. Hold one Microsoft Teams review each month, recorded and transcribed, so decisions and responsibilities remain clear.

03

Priority practice areas

The first paid and publishing effort follows the practice priorities in the accepted strategy.

First acquisition priority

Family Law

Work will centre on the Separation Options Consult, high-intent Google searches, stronger local service pages, useful solicitor-authored education and referral relationships with psychologists, general practitioners, domestic violence services, barristers and interstate firms.

Second acquisition priority

Wills and Estates

Work will improve estate planning, probate and estate dispute visibility, strengthen useful educational material and support professional relationships with accountants, financial planners and other trusted advisers.

Supported where useful

Commercial and Property

Supported through the firm-wide publishing and referrer system where useful, but not treated as the first paid acquisition build unless priorities change.

A CLEAR RESPONSIBILITY LINE

We handle planning, briefs, editing, design, publishing, campaign management, coordination, follow-up administration and measurement. Solari & Stock retains legal judgment, legal accuracy, client advice, relationship ownership and final approval.
