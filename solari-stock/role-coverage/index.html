2026-2027 | ROLE COVERAGE EXTERNAL MARKETING AND BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT SUPPORT

The role, delivered as an accountable external service

Solari & Stock is seeking practical ownership of marketing, business development, referral relationships, publishing, campaigns and performance reporting. The proposed service supplies that operating function without requiring another full-time in-house appointment.

The accepted Marketing and Business Development Strategy 2026-2027 remains the strategic authority. Our role is to organise, produce, publish, manage and measure the work required to put its marketing and acquisition priorities into operation.

HOW THE ADVERTISED ROLE IS COVERED

Business development and referrals

Maintain the professional contact register, segment priority referrer groups, prepare outreach and meeting material, schedule follow-up, support agreed events and record referred enquiries and matters. Directors and lawyers continue to own the relationships. We make the programme organised, consistent and visible.

Marketing strategy and campaigns

Maintain the master marketing calendar and turn agreed priorities into scheduled work. Build and manage Google Search, Facebook and Instagram campaigns, starting with the Separation Options Consult, and improve advertisements, audiences, landing pages, booking paths and follow-up using actual results.

Website and local search

Own the website work list and coordinate hosting, development and technical suppliers. Improve Family Law and Wills and Estates service pages, local search coverage, the Google Business Profile, internal links, page information and enquiry paths. Preserve effective existing work while removing duplication and fixing proven gaps.

Content, newsletters and social publishing

Prepare article ideas and solicitor briefs, edit approved legal content, create images, publish website material and repurpose it across LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram and email. Produce the monthly client newsletter, quarterly professional and referrer newsletter, targeted professional outreach and agreed guides or brochures.

Reporting and client acquisition tracking

Connect available data from the website, local search, paid campaigns, email, social activity, enquiries and matter outcomes in one live dashboard. Track the path from enquiry to consultation to opened matter and known revenue where the information is available.

Coordination and delivery control

Maintain one live work list covering priorities, owners, drafts, approvals, publication dates, campaign status and next actions. Hold one Microsoft Teams review each month, recorded and transcribed, so decisions and responsibilities remain clear.

PRIORITY PRACTICE AREAS

Family Law

The first acquisition priority. Work will centre on the Separation Options Consult, high-intent Google searches, stronger local service pages, useful solicitor-authored education and referral relationships with psychologists, general practitioners, domestic violence services, barristers and interstate firms.

Wills and Estates

The second acquisition priority. Work will improve estate planning, probate and estate dispute visibility, strengthen useful educational material and support professional relationships with accountants, financial planners and other trusted advisers.

Commercial and Property

Supported through the firm-wide publishing and referrer system where useful, but not treated as the first paid acquisition build unless priorities change.

A CLEAR RESPONSIBILITY LINE

We handle planning, briefs, editing, design, publishing, campaign management, coordination, follow-up administration and measurement. Solari & Stock retains legal judgment, legal accuracy, client advice, relationship ownership and final approval.
