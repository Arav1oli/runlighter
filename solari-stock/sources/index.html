SEARCH METHOD

Google searches were observed on 11 August 2026 using Australian results with personalisation disabled. The queries specified Miranda or Sutherland Shire. Organic positions excluded advertisements, local business results, artificial intelligence summaries, question modules and forum blocks.

The observed results are a single-device, single-moment comparison. Local positions can vary according to the searcher's exact location, device, time, relevance, prominence and profile completeness. Not observed on the first page does not mean a domain never ranks.

No access was available to Google Search Console, Google Analytics reporting, Google Business Profile performance, call records, the practice management system, Logline work logs, backlink tools or any advertising account. Public tags show that measurement tools are installed. They do not prove that enquiries, consultations, opened matters and revenue are configured or reported correctly.
