2026-2027 | DELIVERY SYSTEM FROM APPROVED PRIORITY TO COMPLETED WORK

A clear operating rhythm for marketing and acquisition

The service is designed to keep work moving without requiring an in-house marketing manager. One system will show what is planned, what is waiting for legal approval, what has been published, what campaigns are active and what results are available.

THE DELIVERY FLOW

1. Plan

Maintain a master calendar for articles, newsletters, social publishing, campaigns, professional outreach, guides and agreed events. Each item has an owner, purpose, audience, due date and next action.

2. Prepare

Create article ideas and solicitor briefs, campaign copy, images, newsletter layouts, social adaptations, landing-page content, professional lists and meeting material.

3. Approve

Route legal substance, campaign claims and client-facing material to the nominated solicitor or Director. Record approval and changes in the work list so there is one current version.

4. Publish and run

Publish approved website material, schedule LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram content, send newsletters and outreach, activate approved advertisements and maintain booking and follow-up paths.

5. Track and improve

Bring available website, advertising, email, social, enquiry, referral and matter results into the live dashboard. Use the evidence to improve the next page, message, audience, campaign or follow-up action.

WEEKLY OPERATING RHYTHM

Workstream Weekly output
Article ideation One practical idea and writing brief for the appropriate solicitor.
Website publishing One approved article edited, search-optimised, imaged and published. If legal copy is not ready, complete a priority page or local search improvement instead.
Social publishing Three core content pieces, each adapted and scheduled across LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram.
Advertising One optimisation cycle across active Google Search, Facebook and Instagram campaigns.
Website and local search At least one priority improvement, plus checks of technical issues, the Google Business Profile, local pages and enquiry paths.
Email and referrer follow-up One cycle covering audience lists, scheduled campaigns, replies, next actions and meeting preparation.
Measurement Add new enquiries, known sources, campaign results, referred work and available matter outcomes to the live dashboard.

SCHEDULED DELIVERY

Every month

One fully designed client newsletter, one targeted professional outreach campaign and one Microsoft Teams performance and planning meeting. The meeting is recorded and transcribed so decisions and actions are documented.

Every quarter

One professional and referrer newsletter, one substantial client guide, referrer resource or brochure, and one content and campaign priority review.

Always available

The live performance dashboard, master marketing calendar, current website and campaign work list, approvals and deadlines.

CONTENT SAFETY AND REPURPOSING

Solicitors remain the authors and approvers of legal substance. The delivery team can improve approved material for clarity, length, search visibility and channel fit without replacing the lawyer's legal judgment.

One approved article can be used as:

  • a website article or service-page update;
  • a newsletter feature with tracked links back to the website;
  • LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram content;
  • a short frequently asked question;
  • a Google Business Profile update;
  • a module in a client guide or referrer resource; and
  • a useful follow-up item for a professional contact.

REFERRER WORKFLOW

Priority groups

For Family Law, the priority groups include psychologists, general practitioners, domestic violence services, barristers and interstate firms. For Wills and Estates, the priority groups include accountants, financial planners and other trusted advisers. Relevant property professionals can be supported where agreed.

What the service manages

Maintain contact groups and relationship records, prepare useful outreach, schedule agreed check-ins, issue invitations and newsletters, prepare meeting notes, record replies, set next actions and connect referred enquiries and matters where the information is available.

What remains personal

Directors and lawyers own the professional relationships, provide the expert value and attend the conversations that create trust. The system ensures those relationships are supported consistently and do not depend on memory.

LIVE PERFORMANCE VIEW

Acquisition

Website visits, local search actions, advertising spend, clicks, enquiries, bookings, cost per enquiry, consultations, matters opened and known revenue where the information is available.

Publishing

Articles and page updates, newsletter delivery and link clicks, website visits from email, social publishing and campaign activity.

Referrers

Contacts engaged, replies, meetings, next actions, referred enquiries, opened matters and known revenue where the information is available.

Delivery control

The current work list, content calendar, campaign status, owners, approvals, deadlines and agreed actions from the monthly review.

WORKING MODEL

Day-to-day work is delivered remotely. One Microsoft Teams review is held each month and recorded and transcribed by agreement. On-site attendance is arranged only where it is useful and agreed.

The first three months are a flexible commencement period. At the end of month three, both parties review delivery, available results, priorities and fit. If both parties are satisfied, the preference is to agree a 12-month term so delivery time and resources can be reserved.
