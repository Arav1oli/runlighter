OBSERVED 11 AUGUST 2026 | MIRANDA AND SUTHERLAND SHIRE CURRENT POSITION AND GROWTH EVIDENCE

A strong base with clear room to grow

Solari & Stock already has active publishing, strong immediate-suburb relevance and credible third-party recognition. The opportunity is not to discard that foundation. It is to connect the activity to enquiries and matters, improve broader Sutherland Shire visibility and direct effort towards the work most likely to create qualified Family Law and Wills and Estates clients.

WHAT IS ALREADY WORKING

Immediate Miranda visibility

In the observed searches, Solari & Stock appeared second in both the local business results and organic results for family lawyer Miranda NSW. It appeared third in the local business results and second organically for wills lawyer Miranda NSW.

Active website publishing

The public website sitemap contained 737 indexable post URLs and showed updates through August 2026. Core pages had search titles, descriptions and canonical links in place.

Measurement foundations exist

Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and a Meta Pixel were present in public page source. Their presence provides a base for measurement, although it does not prove that calls, forms, consultations and opened matters are configured and reported as conversions.

Trusted external recognition

The firm has authoritative public mentions from organisations including Doyle's Guide, LexisNexis, Thomson Reuters, the Australian Association of Collaborative Professionals and Southern Sydney Collaborative Professionals.

Strong results reported in the accepted strategy

The accepted strategy reported organic visits increasing from 10,344 in the 2024 financial year to 12,511 in the 2025 financial year, an increase of 21 per cent. It also reported an email open rate of about 89 per cent and Facebook engagement of 3.7 per cent. These are useful activity signals, but source and matter attribution remain the key missing commercial measure.

WHAT THE PATTERN MEANS

Solari & Stock has meaningful relevance in Miranda, but the observed visibility does not yet extend consistently across broader Sutherland Shire searches for Family Law and Wills and Estates. This is not a finding that no search work has been done. It is a narrow local footprint with incomplete category coverage.

There is also a brand-confusion risk. A separate Cronulla firm, Solari Law, held prominent organic positions for broad Sutherland Shire family lawyer and divorce lawyer searches. Clearer Solari & Stock service pages and local search coverage can reduce the chance of an intending client selecting the similarly named competitor.

THE CLEAREST ADDITIONAL GROWTH AREAS

Broader local search coverage

Build and improve service and location paths for family law, separation and divorce, property settlements, estate planning, probate and estate disputes across the Sutherland Shire, without creating thin or repetitive pages.

Better use of existing content

The site has more indexable posts than the observed competitors, but content volume is not producing broad category leadership. The next gain should come from consolidation, search intent, internal links, useful service-page updates and repurposing, not volume for its own sake.

Review prominence

Solari & Stock showed 22 Google reviews in the observed results, compared with 86 for Willis & Bowring, 89 for Southern Waters Legal and 443 for Brander Smith McKnight. A consistent, genuine and compliant review request process would strengthen public trust and local prominence.

Cleaner website structure

The public site included a highly relevant Sutherland Shire family lawyer URL that displayed a generic Contact Us title and heading. It also exposed template pages titled About Our Lab and Sample Page in the page sitemap. These are practical clean-up opportunities.

Complete local business information

Public page code did not expose the richer legal service and local business information used by some competitors. Accurate organisation, legal service, address, location and opening-hours data should be added and validated.

Paid acquisition with full tracking

No Solari & Stock search advertisement was observed during the tested searches. This does not prove that campaigns do not exist. It does show an opportunity to verify current accounts and then build controlled Google Search, Facebook and Instagram campaigns that report enquiries, bookings and available matter outcomes.

Source and conversion visibility

The accepted strategy identified source attribution as the primary blind spot. The key improvement is a live view connecting search, advertising, email and referrals to enquiries, consultations, opened matters and known revenue where the information is available.

CURRENT LOCAL SEARCH PACKAGE

The Logline invoice records $1,150 before GST each month for a Local SEO Package and a separate $350 before GST for WordPress hosting, security certificate management and three development hours. Public evidence shows active maintenance and meaningful Miranda visibility. It does not provide enough information to verify the return on the local search fee or allocate which work was funded by each invoice line.

The recommended approach is to preserve the website and existing rankings, obtain the underlying account data and work records, retain what is effective and remove duplication only after the evidence is understood.
