Automated marketing · Sydney and NSW

Marketing that keeps going when you get busy.

In most service businesses marketing is the first thing dropped when the work picks up. Run Lighter takes the repeatable half — production, scheduling, publishing and reporting — and runs it to a calendar, so the output stops depending on who has a spare afternoon.

What gets automated

The parts that repeat, not the parts that need you.

Positioning, judgement and client relationships stay with you. Everything downstream of an approved idea — drafting, formatting, scheduling, publishing, distributing and measuring — is work that can run to a system instead of a person.

The publishing engine

One approved idea, published everywhere it should be.

We build the production line that turns a decision into published work: the draft, the formats, the schedule and the distribution, all handled without a weekly scramble.

  • Article drafting, formatting and publishing
  • Social posts queued weeks ahead
  • Email campaigns and list hygiene
  • Website and landing page updates
  • Google Business Profile and local search upkeep
We do not publish under your name without you seeing it. Every piece has an approval step, and you can change or kill anything in the queue.
The measurement loop

Know what the marketing actually did.

Most marketing reporting is a screenshot of numbers nobody acts on. We report the same four things every month in the same format, so you can see a trend rather than a snapshot.

  • Enquiries traced back to their source
  • Cost per enquiry by channel
  • What published, and what it did
  • One clear recommendation: stop, keep or try
Reporting is built from the tools you already run. We will not add another subscription unless nothing you have can do the job, and we will say so plainly if that is the case.

Local search and Google Business Profile upkeep

For a Sydney service business the Google Business Profile is often doing more work than the website. We keep it current: services and areas kept accurate, posts published on the same schedule as everything else, questions answered rather than left, and photos added as the work happens.

We will also prompt you when it is a reasonable moment to ask a genuine customer for a review, but we do not write reviews, solicit fake ones or offer anything in exchange for them. If you are deciding where to spend the effort first, which service page to improve first is usually the better question than which channel to add.

Implementation, not another strategy deck.

Most Sydney marketing agencies sell you a strategy and then hand the doing back to you. If you already know roughly what you want to say and who you want to say it to, the strategy is not your problem. The problem is that nobody in the business has three uninterrupted hours a week to actually publish it.

That is the part we take. You bring the priority for the month; we produce, format, schedule, publish, distribute and measure it, and everything passes an approval step with you before it goes out under your name. It is worth being clear about what an external marketing function should actually manage, and about what a marketing report should show beyond clicks once it does.

A good deal of the value is in the machinery rather than the message: we automate the work around marketing so the publishing does not stall, and we hand what it brings in to the follow-up system that catches what the marketing brings in.
