What we discussed

The brief from our first call.

This summary records the main facts Linda and Adrian discussed on 5 August, so the recommendation can be checked against the actual conversation.

01 / CURRENT POSITION

A new business relying on referrals

T&B described the business as quite new. Current acquisition is mainly referrals, direct email and outreach, with no active social pages or paid advertising program.

02 / DESIRED SUPPORT

A complete, managed package

Linda was open to seeing a package covering account setup, ongoing organic content, social advertising and the systems that sit behind each incoming lead.

03 / STARTING BUDGET

$500 a month for Meta testing

Linda confirmed that $500 per month paid to Meta was a comfortable starting media budget for Facebook and Instagram advertising.

04 / LEAD CAPACITY

The team can handle conversations

T&B has an administration resource able to process enquiries. Linda indicated that two or three prospective-client conversations per day would be welcome rather than overwhelming.

05 / EXISTING SYSTEM

GoHighLevel is already in place

The website enquiry pop-up feeds the existing GoHighLevel setup. T&B does not currently use a separate email marketing platform or structured nurture sequence.

06 / INTENDED OUTCOME

Build a repeatable path beyond referrals

The immediate aim is to establish public credibility, generate new enquiries and make sure leads are delivered and followed up—not left sitting inside an advertising platform.
