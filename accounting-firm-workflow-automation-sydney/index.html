The implementation method

Start with a completed case, not a software demo.

The first proof is deliberately bounded. The aim is evidence that one handoff works in your environment—not a dramatic platform replacement.

01

Trace

Use one completed example to identify the real trigger, systems, decisions, owner and finish state.

02

Bound

Separate routine steps from professional judgement, commercial decisions and unusual exceptions.

03

Prove

Implement one safe path with logs, permissions, stop conditions and a human review point.

04

Read back

Confirm the result in the destination system and compare it with the completed case before expanding.

Human control

What can move automatically—and what should not.

A workflow can prepare and route information. It should not hide accountability behind a confidence score.

Good automation work

  • Capture structured information once
  • Create a review task in the approved system
  • Check completeness and route exceptions
  • Send an approved reminder from a known status
  • Record the outcome and timestamp

Keep a person responsible

  • Client acceptance and risk decisions
  • Professional advice and sign-off
  • Fee, scope and relationship judgement
  • Security or privacy exceptions
  • Anything the evidence cannot support
