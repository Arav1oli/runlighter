Italian homewares.
A little closer to where we come from.

Our developing homewares brand is inspired by Italian heritage and the familiar pleasure of making, sharing and gathering. It begins with the everyday rituals that help us feel connected to where we come from.

Useful objects for hands in dough, a place at the table and a quiet pause at home. A way to keep inherited customs alive in modern daily life.
