Tavola

Italian for “table”

The sharing board

An invitation to gather around something good. Tavola explores a simple board that brings food to the table and makes room for sharing.

The ritual

Put something
in the middle.

Putting food in the middle.

A meal can begin with a small gesture: setting food within reach and inviting someone to help themselves. Tavola is an idea for that moment, whether there are two people or a few more.

About this design study

This design study explores a wooden serving board with a generous shared surface and an easy-to-hold end. Its scale, materials and final details are still being developed.

An everyday invitation

I

Bring it to the table.

Think less about individual portions and more about a shared beginning.

II

Make room.

A familiar meal can become an occasion simply by welcoming someone in.

III

Keep it everyday.

Bread, a small bite or a meal together. Sharing does not need a special day.
