The ritual

A little dough.
A place to begin.

Making something with our hands.

The first piece is never quite the same as the next. That is part of the pleasure. A small board gives the hands somewhere to work, and a familiar gesture somewhere to return.

About this design study

This design study explores a compact wooden pasta board with a ridged working surface and a simple handle. Its scale, materials and final details are still being developed.

An everyday invitation

I

Start small.

A little dough and one new gesture can be enough to begin.

II

Make it together.

Leave room for another pair of hands, a question or a shared attempt.

III

Let it be yours.

The uneven pieces can carry the pleasure of having made them yourself.
