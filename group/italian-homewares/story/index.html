Where it begins

Italian heritage.
Everyday human connection.

Some things feel familiar
before we can explain why.

There is a particular pleasure in doing something with our hands. Pressing dough. Tending soil. Cooking over a fire. Putting food on a table and asking someone to stay.

We can live very different lives from the people who came before us and still recognise something in these gestures. A sense of participation. A feeling of belonging.

Italian heritage is the starting point for this collection. Florence, the land around it, and the everyday customs of making and gathering give shape to the idea. We are interested in the feeling those customs carry, and how it can belong in a home today.

We return to what is already within us.

A tradition can be
something we do today.

For us, heritage is an invitation to take part. It can be as simple as learning to roll a piece of pasta, carrying a board of food to the table, or making time for a cup and a conversation.

The home does not need to be large. The occasion does not need to be special. We are thinking about practical objects that make these small moments easier to begin and more enjoyable to share.

A return to
everyday rituals.

Our idea is an invitation to return to familiar gestures, useful things and time with one another. The brand name is still in development. Our purpose is a little closer to where we come from.

We are developing our first collection around three rituals: making, sharing and pausing. Fatto, Tavola and Pausa are early design studies, with the finished products still to come.

What guides us

I

Useful, first.

The object should give someone a reason to use it, learn with it and make it part of daily life.

II

Room for real life.

We think about modern homes, limited space and the everyday moments between special occasions.

III

Connection is the purpose.

Italian inspiration opens the door. The pleasure of making and sharing belongs to everyone.

Distinct identities. A shared foundation.

Our spirit is our own.
Our support is shared.
